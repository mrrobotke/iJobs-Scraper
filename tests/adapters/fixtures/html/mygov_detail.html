Chief Economist

Position Summary

The National Treasury seeks to recruit a Chief Economist to lead the macroeconomic policy division. The successful candidate will oversee fiscal planning and economic research.

How to Apply

Applications should be submitted to the National Treasury offices or via email to recruitment@treasury.go.ke.
